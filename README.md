## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an aspirational professional portfolio that represents the direction I am moving toward as a Project Management and Operations professional.

The goal of this site is to communicate:
- the kind of work I do
- the systems I build
- the problems I solve
- and how I approach project management with clarity and structure

Rather than presenting a traditional resume, this portfolio is designed as a structured, intentional representation of my professional identity.

---

## 🎯 Professional Direction
I am focused on project management, operations, and system-building.

My work centers around:
- organizing complex workflows
- aligning teams across functions
- tracking performance through data
- improving efficiency through structured systems

## 🧱 Portfolio Structure
The site is built as a one-page scrolling experience with the following sections:

- Hero (introduction and positioning)
- Philosophy (my approach to project management)
- Experience Snapshot (high-level impact metrics)
- Current Role (PIM Brands internship)
- Systems I Build (real work translated into systems)
- Skills & Tools
- AI Workflow
- Future Direction
- Contact

---

## 🎨 Design Style
This portfolio uses an **Editorial Modernism** design style.

This is reflected in:
- clean layout
- minimal color palette
- strong typography
- structured spacing
- clear hierarchy of information

The goal is to create a professional, high-end, and focused experience.

---

## 🧠 Brand Archetype
The site is built around **The Sage** archetype.

This represents:
- clarity
- structure
- calm leadership
- strategic thinking
- guiding complex work toward execution

This archetype influenced the tone, layout, and messaging throughout the site.

---

## 🧲 Persuasion Principle (Cialdini)
This portfolio uses the principle of **Authority**.

This is shown through:
- real metrics and results
- structured presentation of experience
- confident and direct language
- emphasis on systems and outcomes

The goal is to build credibility and trust with the viewer.
